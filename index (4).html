Crutches, walkers and wheelchairs, back in motion.

ReMobilize collects used mobility equipment from Houston homes, clinics and communities, gives every piece a basic check and cleaning, and passes it to local charities that get it to people who can't afford it.

How it works

From your closet to someone who needs it

Most mobility equipment is used for a few weeks after an injury or surgery, then sits unused for years. We put it back to work.

01

You reach out

Tell us what you have. We can usually pick up anywhere in the Houston area, or you can drop off at one of our collection drives.

02

We check and clean it

Our volunteers give each item a basic check and cleaning. Anything clearly broken or worn out is recycled instead of passed on.

03

A partner gets it to a neighbor

We deliver equipment to Houston charities, shelters and clinics, who confirm fit and condition before giving it to someone who needs it.

What we take

Gently used and working

If it still does its job and you'd trust a family member to use it, we'd love to have it.

  • Crutches
  • Walkers
  • Rollators
  • Canes
  • Manual wheelchairs
  • Knee scooters

Before you donate

Please skip anything broken, missing parts or recalled.

Have something larger, like a power wheelchair or hospital bed? Let us know first so we can make sure we bring the right transport.

Partner with us

Clinics, congregations and community groups

A few partners can move a lot of equipment. We make it easy on your team.

Clinics and care facilities

Set aside the crutches, walkers and other equipment patients leave behind or want to donate. When you have a few items, send us a note and we'll pick them up in bulk.

  • No bin or lobby space needed
  • Pickup on your schedule
  • A collection bin is available if you prefer

Congregations, clubs and neighborhoods

Help us reach the people who have equipment sitting in a garage.

  • Share a short announcement with your members
  • Host a one-day drop-off drive with our volunteers
  • Let us know when items come in and we'll collect them

Charities that serve people in need

If your clients need mobility equipment, we'd like to supply you. Tell us what you need most, and we'll collect with you in mind.

Volunteers

We need help with pickups, cleaning and drives. Students and community members are welcome.
